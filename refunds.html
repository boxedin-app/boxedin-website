Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: November 6, 2026. The full rules are in our Terms of Service, sections 7 to 9.

Cancel anytime

Memberships renew automatically each year until you cancel. Canceling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

  • Paid on our website: cancel anytime online at boxedinnyc.com/manage or in the app. You keep BoxedIn until the end of your paid time.
  • Paid in the app through Apple or Google: cancel in your iPhone or Android subscription settings. The Manage membership button takes you there.
Membership refunds
  • Bought on our website: no refunds for partial periods. Billing mistakes are always fixed; contact support@boxedinnyc.com.
  • Exception: if your renewal price goes up, you can cancel within 14 days after that charge and get a prorated refund for the unused time.
  • Bought in the app: refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their refund policies.
  • We email you about 25 days before every yearly renewal, with the price for the coming year.
Stickers

Sticker orders are final once paid, with these exceptions:

  • A sticker that arrives damaged or doesn't scan: contact us within 14 days and we'll replace it with a complimentary sticker.
  • An order we can't ship within the time we promised: we refund it.

Replacement stickers: your first one every 12 months is complimentary, then $6.99 each.

Questions? support@boxedinnyc.com. We reply within 1 business day.
