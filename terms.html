Community Guidelines

BoxedIn exists so neighbors can solve a blocked car in minutes, without honking, waiting, or fighting. These guidelines apply to everyone: owners, people who scan, and our team.

Be respectful

  • Treat the other person as a neighbor having a bad day, not an enemy.
  • Keep replies short and polite. No insults, threats, slurs, or sexual comments.
  • Don't discriminate against anyone for race, religion, national origin, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, or any other protected characteristic.

Stay safe

  • Call 911 first if you feel unsafe or anyone is threatened or hurt.
  • Never confront. Don't wait by the car to argue, block the other car back, or follow anyone.
  • Don't touch, damage, mark, or try to move someone else's vehicle.

Send honest alerts

  • Scan only when you're actually blocked in.
  • One scan is enough. BoxedIn sends reminders for you.
  • If you're the owner, move as soon as you safely can, or reply with how long you'll be.

Respect privacy

  • Don't use BoxedIn to find out who someone is or where they live.
  • Don't post screenshots, plates, faces, or recordings of other users to shame them.
  • Keep your own account to yourself. No shared logins, no stickers on cars that aren't yours.

Recording

You can record for your own safety where the law allows. Use it to protect yourself or to support a report, never to embarrass someone online.

Follow the law

Obey parking, traffic, and local laws. BoxedIn never gives anyone permission to double-park or block another vehicle.

Answer your alerts (Good Neighbor rules)

Only alerts you never answer count against you. If you replied, you're in good standing, even if your neighbor forgets to tap "I'm out."

  • A missed alert counts only if it reached your phone and you didn't reply within 10 minutes. Alerts that failed to deliver never count.
  • 3 missed alerts in 30 days: we send you a friendly warning.
  • 5 missed alerts in 45 days: your sticker is paused for 30 days.
  • Missed alerts drop off your record after 90 days.
  • You can see your own missed alerts in the app and dispute any of them with one tap. A BoxedIn support representative reviews every dispute.

If it keeps happening:

  • A 2nd pause within 12 months: your sticker is paused for 90 days, with a last-chance notice.
  • A 3rd pause within 12 months: a BoxedIn support representative reviews your account, and it may be closed for good.

Zero tolerance (one strike)

Some things end your use of BoxedIn the first time.

  • Threats, harassment, stalking, hate, or using BoxedIn to find or follow someone: your account is suspended right away while a BoxedIn support representative reviews it. If confirmed, the ban is permanent, and we may share the case with law enforcement when the law requires it.
  • Fake or prank alerts: 3 confirmed fake alerts block that phone from sending alerts for 30 days. If it happens again, the block is permanent.
  • A banned person may not create a new account. Our ID check helps us enforce this.

Every permanent ban is decided by a BoxedIn support representative, never automatically. You can appeal once by contacting support@boxedinnyc.com.

What happens if guidelines are broken

We review reports and may warn, limit, suspend, or close accounts and deactivate stickers. Serious safety issues may be shared with law enforcement when required by law. Repeat fake alerts from the same phone may be blocked.
